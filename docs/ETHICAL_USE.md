# Ethical Use Statement — Micelia

**Status**: declarative statement of values, NOT a legal license restriction
**Effective**: 2026-05-24
**Companion to**: `LICENSE` (AGPLv3) — this document does not modify or restrict the AGPLv3 grant

---

## Why this document exists separately from the LICENSE

Some projects embed ethical clauses directly into their license (Hippocratic License, Anti-Capitalist Software License). We considered that approach and chose not to. Two reasons:

1. **Legal enforceability**: ethical-clause licenses have weak track records in court. Adding them weakens the AGPL enforcement we actually want.
2. **OSI compatibility**: licenses with field-of-use restrictions are not "open source" by OSI definition. Losing that status would harm Micelia's distribution and credibility more than the marginal protection an ethical clause provides.

Instead, we publish this Ethical Use Statement as a separate declaration. It articulates the values of the Micelia project. It is not legally binding on you. It is binding on us — the Steward and the Active Nodes — in how we develop, prioritize, and govern Micelia.

If you find these values compelling, we hope you use Micelia in ways consistent with them. If you find them objectionable, we hope you fork the project under a different name (see `docs/TRADEMARK_POLICY.md`) rather than distorting what "Micelia" means.

## What Micelia is for

Micelia is infrastructure for a **cooperative socioeconomic system** in which individuals contribute to collective progress through AI-assisted work and receive coverage of basic needs calibrated to their contribution. It exists because the dominant transitions of the AI era — surveillance capitalism, walled-garden subsidies, tecnofeudalism — are not the only options.

Concretely, Micelia is designed to serve:

- **Individuals** who want agency over their work, their data, their compute, and their AI tools
- **Cooperatives** that need shared infrastructure without vendor lock-in
- **Public institutions** seeking measurable alternatives to passive social transfers
- **Researchers** whose work benefits from reproducible AI-assisted scientific method
- **Communities** that want to federate their tools without joining a centralized platform

---

## What Micelia is not for

We did not build Micelia for these uses, and we ask you to consider whether other tools fit better:

- **Mass surveillance** of populations, employees, or specific groups
- **Generation of non-consensual sexual imagery**, including content depicting real persons without their explicit informed consent
- **Automated denial of essential services** to individuals based on opaque algorithmic scoring (insurance denial, credit denial, parole denial, welfare denial)
- **Disinformation campaigns** designed to manipulate democratic processes
- **Weapons systems** of any kind, including dual-use systems where weapons application is foreseeable
- **Production of CSAM** or any content that sexualizes minors, in any form
- **Wage suppression schemes** that use cooperative aesthetics to obscure exploitative labor arrangements
- **Tax evasion infrastructure** disguised as "decentralized finance"
- **Extractive scraping of cooperatively-produced data** for proprietary AI training (see also `docs/AI_SOVEREIGNTY_POLICY.md` §2.4)

This is not an exhaustive list. It is a sample of uses that we, the project's contributors, consider incompatible with what Micelia is meant to do.

## What we ask of users

These are requests, not requirements:

1. **Read this document before deploying Micelia at scale.** If you plan to operate a node serving more than 100 users, take 10 minutes to ensure your use is aligned.
2. **If your use sits in a gray area, talk to us.** Open a discussion in the repository. We don't have a vendor approval process — we have a community happy to think with you.
3. **Don't use the Micelia name for uses listed in "what Micelia is not for".** This is one place where the request strengthens: per `docs/TRADEMARK_POLICY.md`, such uses fail the fork test and require renaming.
4. **Contribute back what you learn.** Even if you don't contribute code, sharing how you deploy Micelia helps the cooperative evolve.

---

## What we commit to

These are commitments of the Steward and the Active Nodes:

1. **Prioritize the use cases listed in "what Micelia is for"** in roadmap, documentation, and support.
2. **Refuse partnerships, sponsorships, and contracts whose primary purpose conflicts with this statement.** This includes refusing money, even when it is offered without strings attached, if accepting it would create reputational alignment with a misuse of Micelia.
3. **Document and reject** PRs whose purpose is to enable a misuse listed above, even when those PRs are technically clean.
4. **Be transparent** about edge cases and gray areas. When we make a judgment call, we publish it.

---

## On values, not virtue signaling

The values in this document are not exhaustive of what we care about. They are the ones that have actionable implications for Micelia development. We resist the temptation to expand this list into a manifesto. A short, sincere, enforceable list of values serves the project better than a long aspirational one.

If you disagree with these values, that is a legitimate position. The cooperative tradition includes both projects that embed strong ethical commitments and projects that take a more libertarian stance toward end use. Micelia takes the former position because its founders believe the AI transition makes the alternative — a posture of strict neutrality — implicitly complicit in whatever uses dominant capital chooses.

We may be wrong. If you think we are wrong, write to us.
